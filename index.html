About

I work in software and like building small, useful tools — the kind of habit that eventually turns into a GitHub account full of half-finished side projects. This one, gregrushton.com, is where the finished ones land.

Outside of that, I'm a beekeeper and genealogist by hobby: parish registers, and the occasional afternoon lost in a scanned 19th-century ledger.
